PRIVACY NOTICE.

How your information is used when you browse and order.

ORDER INFORMATION

Checkout collects your name, email address and phone number. Delivery orders also collect your address. These details are stored with your order so we can arrange payment, fulfilment and customer support. Account passwords are stored as salted hashes, and sign-in sessions are stored separately. Order details are accessible through the private admin area.

Your bag is saved in your browser. During checkout, this tab may temporarily retain an order attempt so a failed connection can be retried without creating a duplicate order. Card details are entered on the selected payment provider?s website, rather than collected by this store. Online checkout shares your email address, order reference and total with the provider; the store retains payment identifiers and confirmation status.

Card images and font resources may load from third-party providers. Your browser and hosting provider may process standard technical requests and logs.

WEBSITE TRAFFIC

We measure page views, estimated visits, popular pages, referring website domains and general device categories to understand how our site is used. A random identifier in your browser tab groups page views into visits and resets after 30 minutes without a tracked page view. This tracking uses no analytics cookies and does not store names, email addresses, IP addresses, full referring URLs or page query parameters. Visitor analytics are stored on our own database and shown only in the private admin area. Records are retained for up to 95 days, with older records removed during periodic cleanup. We honour Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control browser signals.
