GOOD TO KNOW.

Answers about the preview and what we're building for launch.

Can I place an order now?+

Not yet. PokéMota is a read-only preview. Checkout, order creation and payments are disabled.

Are the listed cards available?+

No availability is implied. Every current product, stock quantity and price is demonstration data. We will publish verified listings before launch.

Are the product images actual card photographs?+

No. Current artwork is intentionally decorative placeholder art. Authentic card photographs will be added to verified listings later.

Where is PokéMota based?+

PokéMota is a collector-owned South African project.

What conditions will you offer?+

Condition labels in this preview are illustrative. We plan to clearly describe and photograph the condition of individual cards before accepting orders.

Will you deliver across South Africa?+

We're planning local courier options. Exact coverage, delivery fees and timelines are not finalized.

Is PokéMota affiliated with Pokémon?+

PokéMota is an independent collector-store project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo, The Pokémon Company or Game Freak.
