GOOD TO KNOW.

Answers about the preview and what we're building for launch.

How do I order?+

Add published products to your bag and complete checkout. Your order is saved as awaiting EFT payment.

How do I pay?+

Our banking details and payment instructions appear after checkout saves your order. Pay the order total by EFT using your order reference as the payment reference. We confirm payment before arranging fulfilment.

What does delivery cost?+

Delivery within South Africa is R80 per order. Collection in Brits is free and arranged after payment confirmation.

Are the card images photos of the stock?+

API images are reference scans of the card printing. Check the condition listed for your item.

Is PokéMota affiliated with Pokémon?+

PokéMota is an independent collector store and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo, The Pokémon Company or Game Freak.
